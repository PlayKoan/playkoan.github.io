Words of the rule language
How rules join, and what is free

not X, X and Y and Z or X or Y or Z, up to 4 conditions. Never mix and with or in one rule: there are no brackets. Compare rules side by side with vs: has 3 vs sum even vs same ends.

Check is free: it says whether a rule agrees with every koan on the board, and what the rule means. Guess: if your rule disagrees with the board it is bounced, free. If it fits the board but is wrong, it costs 3 and a koan where the two rules differ joins the board. Only the first 10 wrong guesses cost 3.

Tiers 1 to 4 draw the hidden rule from the lists below; tiers 1 to 3 never join conditions. A solve at or under par offers the next tier, and you can start any tier at any time.

The tier lists (tiers 1 to 4)
Tier 5, the open tier

The hidden rule is not from a list. With chance 1 in 4 it is one condition of the tier 3 list, with 1 in 2 two of them joined by and, with 1 in 4 two joined by or.

Weights. Only koans of length 1 to 4 count: a koan of length 1 weighs 64, of length 2 weighs 16, of length 3 weighs 4, of length 4 weighs 1. Each length weighs 256 in all, so everything weighs 1024. A joined rule is drawn only if: (1) its YES koans weigh 205 to 819; (2) it differs from every one-part rule (the conditions of tiers 1 to 3, the not of each, and the nots the lists leave out: not count D exactly 1, not kinds exactly 2 or 3, not more D than E, which are never halves) on koans weighing at least 205; (3) it says YES on at least 2 koans of length 2 to 4, and NO on at least 2.

Two shortcuts. In A and B, the koans where A says YES weigh at least 410, and so do B's. In A or B, the koans where A says YES and B says NO weigh at least 205, and so do those where B says YES and A says NO.

The start has a trap: a condition of tiers 1 to 3, written without not, that fits the four starting koans. It can be one half of the hidden rule, never the whole rule. An apprentice hunts the same puzzle, and the end card shows its score.

About KOAN

KOAN is a puzzle about a hidden rule. The rule says YES or NO to every koan, a row of 1 to 6 digits, each 1 to 4. You see four koans with their answers, test more koans (1 point each), then name the rule in a small word language, such as has 3 or same ends. A wrong guess costs 3. Lower scores are better.

A link can name a puzzle, so a friend gets the same one: #seed=3-48213, #daily=2026-11-14 (the daily follows the GMT date) or #code=D-WRBD-QM0B-BA50-A67D.

Each day has three puzzles, the same for everyone: easy (tier 1), medium (tier 2) and hard (tier 3, the daily). Open New puzzle to play them. Play by link offers the same three.
